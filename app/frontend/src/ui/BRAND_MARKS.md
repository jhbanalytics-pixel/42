# Platform marks

The platform marks in `PlatformLogo.jsx` are the official marks from the
simple-icons package, version 13.21.0, released under the CC0-1.0 licence.
Only the `d` attribute of each mark's single path is copied, on its 24 by 24
grid, and each brand colour is the hex that package gives for it. Nothing is
fetched at run time and the package is not a dependency.

The marks are trademarks of their owners. They are used here only to identify
the platform a list comes from, and the platform's name always sits beside
each one as text.

Marks vendored: Apple Music, Spotify, Shazam, App Store, Google Play, TikTok,
YouTube, Google, Reddit, Instagram and X.

Boomplay and Nairaland are absent from simple-icons 13.21.0, so each keeps the
text monogram until an official mark is supplied by its owner.
