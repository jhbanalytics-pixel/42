# Jo acceptance: Console Workbench research

Gold standard deliverable: Jo's **AI Search Africa Brief [NG & KE]** (June 2026). Console Research output should mirror its section ladder: Objective (problem + metrics), Know the user (background), The tension (named frame), The goal, Know the magic (product fit), Get to by, brand safety, mandatories, how we judge the work, then paste-ready Google AI Mode prompts. Grounded lanes keep `evidence_indices`; inference lanes stay badge-labelled.

Use the live 42 URL with your passcode. Route: `#/console?work=brief`.

## Before you start

1. Open `#/console`. Confirm the workbench shows Ask, Build cited brief, and Reopen work.
2. Open `#/console?work=brief` directly. Confirm the same console shell stays visible and no separate Research app appears.
3. Open an old `#/research` link. Confirm it redirects into console brief mode.

## Generate flow

1. On `#/console?work=brief`, confirm one enabled persona appears (Audience neutral).
2. Confirm the form uses strategist copy: choose the strategic lens, not internal "Jo-style" or admin wording.
3. Select markets (KE, NG, ZA). Tap **Generate research doc**.
4. Watch progress steps: seeds, engine signal, synthesis.
5. When the doc loads, scroll the full page. Confirm you can reach the refine bar at the bottom.

## Doc quality

1. **Objective** and **Know the user** sections show numbered citation chips.
2. Tap a citation chip. Drawer opens with evidence text (not empty).
3. If signal is strong enough, **The tension**, **The goal**, **Know the magic**, **Get to by**, and **Google AI Mode actions** render.
4. Evidence Bank appears and surfaces support by behaviour/market. Tapping an evidence card opens the proof drawer.
5. Activation actions lead with user friction and a clear role for the selected product frame. If the idea could work without that product, fail it.
6. **Copy markdown** and **Copy all prompts** work when those sections exist.

## Share and deep links

1. After generate, URL updates to `#/console?work=brief&artifact=ra_...`.
2. Copy share link, open in a new tab. Same doc reloads with citations intact.
3. From Seeds, **Generate research doc** opens `#/console?work=brief&persona_id=...&markets=...` and auto-starts.
4. The old `#/research?artifact=ra_...` URL still opens the same doc.

## Refine

1. Use a refine chip or type an instruction. Doc updates in place.
2. Citations still work after refine.

## Degraded path (optional)

If a source read times out, a banner explains degraded mode. Doc still renders from engine signal.

## Pass criteria

All generate, scroll, citation, share, and refine checks pass on one persona across at least two markets.

Report failures with: URL, persona, markets, screenshot, and whether artifact id was in the URL.
