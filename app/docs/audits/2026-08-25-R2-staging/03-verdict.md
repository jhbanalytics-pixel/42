# Verdict: redesign from purpose

Current staging fails at 8/30. It is a technically cleaner version of the old product, not the promised intelligence redesign. Accessibility and performance now pass, but the information architecture, signal model, evidence model, copy, and visual grammar still encode the retired Brand24 and fixed-keyword world.

Highest-leverage moves:

1. Honest: make dynamic discovered signals the board identity. Fixed taxonomy becomes optional tagging, and every audience or demographic label states measured or inferred, source, window, and confidence.
2. Useful: make Today one decision path: what changed, why now, proof, possible response. Remove duplicate rankings and place interpretation beside the signal.
3. Minimal: reduce the product to three visual grammars: editorial lead, compact comparison row, and evidence drawer. No generic status-and-metrics card survives.
4. Understandable: replace every engine field with a strategist meaning. Novelty, breadth, source independence, history, evidence readiness, and audience fit replace graph score and raw index precision.
5. Thorough: make SocialCrawl Source Lab, historical analogues, evidence readiness, source quality, and credit use visible so trust comes from proof rather than product copy.

Preserve the editorial brand, job navigation, lead-story sequence, accessibility, performance, themes, focus behavior, direct evidence links, Compare, and the cited brief workflow. Rebuild everything else against the open-discovery foundation.

## Open-question contract

42 is not an election tool, campaign tool, Gen Z tool, or fixed-topic dashboard. It is an open cultural-intelligence workspace over owned and approved public evidence. A strategist asks a natural-language question. 42 identifies the decision, missing scope, markets, time window, audience lenses, source plan, historical comparison, evidence standard, and output form.

The question router must support landscape, explanation, comparison, trajectory, audience, creator, brand role, whitespace, risk, historical analogue, campaign opportunity, source coverage, and custom questions. These are routing intents, not templates that limit the answer.

The only hard boundary is evidence honesty. If the data cannot support a population, demographic, causal, or predictive claim, 42 states the limitation and gives the strongest supported answer instead.

## One golden task in a wider test suite

One mandatory test asks what local people are saying about an upcoming election, which expressed factors are associated with willingness to vote, and what role people expect brands to play. The strategist should not need to choose an engine topic, inspect a graph score, or assemble the answer across routes.

The result is one cited intelligence brief with scope, time window, local conversation themes, expressed decision drivers, audience differences, expected brand roles, existing brand behavior where evidenced, misinformation or polarization risks, evidence readiness, known gaps, and recommended content territories. It must state that social conversation is not representative polling and must not present associations as population-level causes.
