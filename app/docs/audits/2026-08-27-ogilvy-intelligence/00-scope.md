# 42 Ogilvy Intelligence design audit scope

Date: 27 August 2026

Outcome: redesign

The audit covers the authenticated staging experience at desktop and 390 pixel mobile width, the passcode gate, masthead, Today, Explore, Compare, Build, Intelligence Console, Research, footer, theme controls, motion controls, and the shared visual system.

The primary user is an Ogilvy strategist who needs to detect what matters, understand why, inspect proof, compare options, and move one idea into a brief.

The audit judges whether the product:

1. Looks and behaves like an Ogilvy intelligence product.
2. Makes the evidence chain visible without exposing engine internals.
3. Moves from discovery to one decision with low cognitive load.
4. Treats unsupported audience claims, thin evidence, and empty data honestly.
5. Has a distinctive visual idea that survives beyond one fashionable interface trend.

The audit does not judge the quality of the engine algorithms. It does judge whether the interface represents their claims honestly.
