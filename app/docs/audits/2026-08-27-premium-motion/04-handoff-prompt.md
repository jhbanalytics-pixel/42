# Handoff prompt

    Redesign directive: Refine 42 / Ogilvy Intelligence based on a Dieter Rams audit at 23/30.

    Verdict:
    The product structure is useful, understandable and honest, so another redesign would destroy good work. The failure is the last 20% of art direction and interaction craft: typography is generic, the Red Thread is static, and section transitions do not yet feel authored.

    Keep:
    1. One decision path from signal through proof to response.
    2. Explicit evidence readiness and unavailable states.
    3. Black, warm paper and Ogilvy red.
    4. Zero-radius structural layout.
    5. Four working jobs and market scope.

    Fix in priority order:
    1. Use installed OgilvyJBaskerville for display and chapter titles.
    2. Turn the Red Thread into semantic chapter progress.
    3. Add one title interaction, one receipt interaction and one chapter transition.
    4. Keep the lead sticky on desktop while evidence chapters advance.
    5. Reduce repeated red rules.

    Approved interaction direction: Live Margin.

    Motion:
    1. Native smooth anchor scroll.
    2. IntersectionObserver active chapter.
    3. Transform-only thread progress.
    4. Title underline 220ms.
    5. Number and receipt shift 180ms.
    6. Reduced motion uses instant navigation and zero-duration transitions.

    Reject:
    1. Scroll hijacking.
    2. Parallax.
    3. Cursor followers.
    4. Scale or lift.
    5. Glow, shadow, glass, blur or gradient.
    6. Generic Google Fonts.
    7. Motion without data, navigation or focus meaning.

    Required proof:
    1. Actual component render at 1,024, 736 and 390 pixels.
    2. No horizontal overflow.
    3. Every evidence link at least 48 pixels.
    4. Hover and focus parity.
    5. Touch does not depend on hover.
    6. Reduced-motion equivalence.
    7. Smooth-scroll fallback.
    8. No idle animation.
