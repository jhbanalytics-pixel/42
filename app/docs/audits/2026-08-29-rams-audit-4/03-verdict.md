## Where the two remaining points sit

Innovation (2/3): the thread marks reading position but not yet evidence relationship. When two sources agreeing, a signal crossing markets, or history repeating each mark the thread, this becomes the instrument the constitution described.

Aesthetic (2/3): the approved Ogilvy faces are unavailable, so display type runs on the Baskerville fallback, and three legacy stylesheets still carry the old grammar for routes that have not migrated.

Long-lasting (2/3) and as-little-design (2/3) are the same debt seen from two sides: eleven destinations remain that the five-job architecture does not want, and six surfaces are refusal stubs whose real shape is unproven.
