# Trends Engine V3 Blueprint

Status: strategy plus architecture, for Albert to act on. V3 evolves V2; it does not replace it. Every code claim below is grounded against `master` as of this writing snapshot (2026-06-30). Where a fact can drift (file counts, flag states), it is stated as approximate-and-current, not as a permanent constant.

## 1. Vision

V3 is V2 with the PULSE Intelligence Core promoted from a paused shadow table that renders nothing into the mandatory pass every reader-facing claim crosses before it reaches a human. V2 answers "what is loud" (the composite `trend_score` ranks topics by weighted momentum). V3 answers "what is true, on what evidence, as of when," and it refuses to assert anything it cannot defend. The contract becomes: every number is computed by code not by a model, every factual sentence carries a clickable receipt bound to specific source rows, every forward claim is a hedged outlook chip or it is cut, and silence beats a guess. The defensible IP is not the model. It is the deterministic trust boundary (a pure-Python corrector that the generative model can only feed, never override) plus the accumulating `event_ledger`, a recency-true cross-source "what was true when" history of SSA Gen Z that no off-the-shelf social tool assembles because those tools optimise mention volume and have no event-state memory and no honesty rail. The product story to BSA and every white-label client after it: we do not show you a trend until the engine can defend it, and we show you exactly why we believe it.

## 2. The problem with V2 today

Four real limits, grounded in the V2 maps, not hypotheticals.

The stale-read trust break is the original sin. THE READ once showed a football match as upcoming after the team had already played and won, because the Brand24 ai-insights grounding returns a weekly retrospective regardless of the requested window and the renderer surfaced it raw. Nothing in `trend_brief.py` or `daily_summary.py` checks whether a dated claim is still in the future. The fix shipped at the time was to remove the Brand24 weekly card (LP #39/#40), not to reconcile the claim. Demoting stale content is not removing it.

Accuracy is not yet a property of the engine. The brief prompt in `src/analysis/prompts/trend_brief.py` is told to ground every claim and not invent, but nothing verifies it did. The honesty rails (the foreign-collision rule, "say the data is thin honestly") are soft prose constraints on the LLM. Only headline emptiness (`assert_sourced`) and the response schema are hard-enforced. The truthfulness of any sentence inside `description_rationale` or `summary_text` is never checked against the underlying rows at render time. This is the future-tense gap specifically: nothing checks whether a dated claim is still in the future. It is distinct from the existing `daily_summary` HARD PICK velocity rule, which governs which topic gets picked, not whether that topic's prose has gone stale.

There is no real pattern intelligence. The engine reasons about `trend_score` velocity and a birth/growth/maturity/decline lifecycle, but it has no model of a discrete real-world event having an outcome (scheduled then happened then resolved). Football is the only event shape with a curated fixture rule; every other event type relies on literal keyword presence. Velocity is pure volume (today vs a 14-day average), so a connector flip or a new RSS feed reads as a cultural surge.

There is no intelligent data navigation. Listening Post retrieval is purely lexical: `search_content` in `bq.py` does token-AND `REGEXP_CONTAINS` with one broadened-OR retry. The M4 multilingual-embedding asset is used only for offline classification rescue, never at query time, so code-switched and slang Gen Z queries (the exact content the keyword classifier caps at ~40%) silently miss.

A substrate fragility sits under all four: watchdogs and live queries hardcode `trends_v2_dev` rather than routing through `get_dataset()`, so a prod move silently reads the wrong table. This is the single biggest foolproofness prerequisite and V3 fixes it first.

## 3. The V3 framework

V3 is layered. Each layer states its role and whether it is reuse or build. The dominant move is reuse; the new code is narrow and additive.

Ingestion (the 8 live connectors plus the `BaseConnector` spine) is the evidence source. Role: unchanged data intake, but every kept row now carries a stable `receipt_id` and a geo-confidence score at ingestion so the spine can bind a claim to exact rows. Reuse `BaseConnector`, `RAW_COLUMNS`/`ENRICHED_COLUMNS`, `safe_fetch`, all vendor normalisers, the `CONNECTORS` registry and the ensemble-before-reddit order, the circuit breakers, the geo-defence primitives. Build small: a `receipt_id` column, a unified per-day EnsembleData token ledger checked against the real 5000 account cap before every call, an idempotent per-(market, source, date) re-ingestion guard, and a cross-connector `geo_confidence` generalising the Reddit foreign-sub exclude.

The `receipt_id` definition has two failure modes that the spec pins down rather than waving past, because a receipt that collides or 404s is worse than no receipt for a trust product. Collisions: many normalised rows (EnsembleData and Brand24 derived feeds, GDELT entity rows) carry no url, so the hash is `sha256(source, content_type, market, url-or-rowtext-fallback, collected_at, intra-batch-sequence)`, where the row-text fallback plus the per-batch sequence number guarantees a urlless row is still unique within its (source, collected_at) group. Dead links: `raw_content` has a 90-day TTL, so a receipt that pointed only at a raw row would 404 once that row aged out. The receipt therefore binds to `enriched_content` (the same TTL-stable surface the embeddings bind to), and the chip degrades to a non-clickable "source aged out" label rather than a live link once the backing enriched row is past retention. The receipt never promises a click it cannot honour.

Classification and composite scoring is the loudness signal. Role: unchanged. `trend_score` still ranks what is moving and the spine sits after it and never touches it, exactly as corroboration does today. Reuse the full layered classifier, the embedding rescue (already live), `_aggregate_by_topic`, `compute_trend_scores`, the channel-family cross-source multiplier, velocity. The channel-family collapse that the trust chip depends on already exists: `_channel_family` in `run_rss_now.py` maps every Brand24 row to a single `brand24` family regardless of its web/facebook/tiktok/instagram platform string (the mapping was fitted against the live source distribution on 2026-06-16), and `corroboration.py` counts `FACTUAL_FAMILIES = {news, search, youtube, music}` over those collapsed families, never over raw platform strings. So V3 reuses the existing family map, it does not build a new one, and a single Brand24 feed already cannot read as multi-source agreement. The one promotion is carrying each row's winning-layer and embedding-cosine confidence into aggregation so a low-confidence keyword graze is a weaker evidence node than a high-confidence multi-channel embedding match.

The Intelligence Core (Section 4) is the new spine: corroboration, event-state ledger, reconcile, and a new per-claim grounding verifier. Mostly reuse, narrow build.

A deterministic relevance re-rank (grafted from the Relevance proposal) sits between scoring and surfacing and is covered in Section 6.

Pattern intelligence detectors (grafted from the Pattern proposal) read the substrate and are covered in Section 5.

Navigation (BigQuery `VECTOR_SEARCH` plus the promoted Listening Post agent) is covered in Section 5.

Surfacing (email, Listening Post, THE READ) becomes a pure consumer of the spine. It renders `claim_receipts`; it does not compute trust. Reuse `render_payload` as the single rehydration contract, the `render_card` binding rule, `confidence_label`, the NOT MEASURED footnote, `assert_sourced`, the LP grounded tool loop. Build the `ConfidenceReceipt` chip, `fetch_ledger_read` in the LP API, the THE READ swap, and a render-time future-tense validator.

Trust observability guards the spine with deterministic recompute checks, the way `composite_integrity` guards `trend_score`. Reuse the `accuracy_watchdog` UNVERIFIED tier and its four trust checks, `reconcile_shadow_digest`, `engine_pulse`/`engine_evolve`, the `predictions_archive` backtest harness, `dry_run_sql`. Build a grounding-faithfulness check and a `detections_archive`-style honest backtest.

## 4. The Intelligence Core

The centerpiece. Three stages plus a grounding verifier, deepened from audit-only into the producer of a new render contract field, `claim_receipts`, that the surfacing layer is hard-gated on. Almost all of it already exists as tested code on `master`.

Stage 1, the corroboration scorer (`src/scoring/corroboration.py`, LIVE and free every cron). Per (market, topic) it emits two separate numbers that are never combined: `factual_corroboration` (news/search/youtube/music family breadth, a GDELT-entity bonus only if `tone_rows>0`, a search-spike bonus, all times an exponential 48h-half-life recency factor) and `social_corroboration`. The tier gate is the deterministic law: "corroborated" requires at least one factual family AND `factual_corroboration>=0.40`, and social-only can NEVER reach corroborated. It is persisted to `trend_scores` every run and does not touch `trend_score`. V3 surfaces this immediately as a receipt, with near-zero risk, because the data already lands.

Stage 2, the event-state ledger (`src/analysis/event_ledger.py`, built, paused). Per (market, `entity_key`) it holds the current resolved state of a watched real-world event (scheduled, ongoing, resolved, evergreen). Anti-hallucination is enforced in Python: Gemini may PROPOSE a state, but it is dropped unless it cites a supplied evidence index pointing at THIS entity's rows, and the deterministic regex guess wins otherwise. Confidence is always computed in Python (`0.4*src + 0.3*recency + 0.3*citation`), never verbalised by the model. V3 deepens it two ways: add a `validity_window` and `current_state` so a newer corroborated state supersedes a stale one (the direct fix for the stale-football bug), and widen `_fetch_factual_rows` from GDELT plus RSS plus search to also feed YouTube and music, which are already in `FACTUAL_FAMILIES` for the scorer but absent from the ledger's evidence fetch today.

Stage 3, reconcile (`src/analysis/reconcile.py`, built, paused, 100% pure deterministic, zero Gemini, imports nothing from the ledger builder). The trust boundary. Per claim it matches entities against the ledger and returns one of four actions: corroborated, stale, labelled, no_match. STALE fires only when the claim implies future AND the matched event is resolved AND the event is fresher than the claim. On stale the require-a-source gate runs: correct to `state_text` verbatim only if the event has at least 2 factual families AND confidence at least 0.55, otherwise label, never correct. Reconcile never writes free text. V3 promotes its output from an audit row into the render contract: it emits, per claim, a `claim_receipt` `{action, matched_entity_key, receipt_ids, confidence_tier, factual_n, social_m, as_of}` that surfacing is hard-gated on, and it runs over the `daily_summary` claims too, not just briefs.

The grounding verifier (new, the faithfulness layer, and the single largest genuinely-new build in V3). Today the brief is told to ground but nothing checks it. There is zero grounding-verifier code on `master` today, so this is a from-scratch component on the cron critical path and it gets its own phase and its own backtest. V3 decomposes each brief into atomic claims and runs each against that topic's actual source rows. Below the citation threshold a sentence is demoted to social-signal phrasing or cut, never asserted as fact. It plugs into the `assert_sourced` seam (extended from value-exists to source-pointer-exists) and the existing `_build_key_metrics` deterministic-override pattern.

Four decisions the build has to pin, because each is a real failure point, not a solved seam:

Model and threshold. There is no named "Vertex check-grounding model" already wired in this repo, so the verifier is a Vertex Gemini call with a structured grounding prompt (claim plus candidate source rows, asked to return supported / partially-supported / unsupported with the supporting row ids), not a magic separate API. The citation threshold is concrete: a claim renders as fact only when at least one returned supporting row id resolves to a real `receipt_id` for THIS topic AND the verdict is "supported"; "partially-supported" demotes to hedged phrasing; "unsupported" or a verdict that cites no resolvable row id cuts the sentence. The per-claim confidence field is unavailable on Gemini 2.5/3.5 Flash, so confidence is computed by our scorer plus the row-id resolution check, never read off the brief model.

Verifier unavailable mid-cron. If the grounding call rate-limits or errors, the claim does not silently pass. It falls back to the deterministic backstop: `key_metrics` (already code-computed and row-bound) render as normal, and any prose claim that could not be verified this run is demoted to hedged social-signal phrasing rather than asserted. A grounding outage degrades the read to "thinner but honest," never to "unverified asserted as fact."

Latency budget. The pass runs across roughly 24 briefs, several atomic claims each. It is batched per topic (all of a topic's claims in one call with that topic's rows), gated to Key-tier and Watch-tier topics first, and shadow-first specifically to measure whether it fits inside the 00:30 UTC run window before it is allowed on the live path. If it does not fit, it stays gated to Key-tier only.

Claim decomposition. This is itself a hard NLP problem, not a solved seam. `key_metrics` are already atomic and bind to a number, so they are trivial. Rationale prose is the risk: a sentence like "X is rising in Lagos because Y dropped a track" carries two claims with potentially different grounding verdicts, and a naive sentence split grounds the conjunction as one unit, so it either passes a half-false sentence or cuts a half-true one. The build splits on sentence and on coordinating/subordinating conjunction boundaries into clause-level units, grounds each independently, and on any decomposition it is unsure about it falls back to grounding the whole sentence and demoting (not cutting) on a mixed verdict, so the failure mode is conservative phrasing, never an asserted half-truth. Decomposition gets its own unit tests against hand-labelled compound sentences.

The architectural principle the whole Core encodes, and the V3 differentiator, is corrector-is-never-the-trust-boundary: the generative model is fenced to PROPOSING and a pure deterministic layer DISPOSES. Accuracy stops being a hope about LLM behaviour and becomes a deterministic gate outside the generator. The accumulating `event_ledger` is the moat, a per-market "what was true when" history that compounds as sources are added.

## 5. Pattern intelligence and intelligent data navigation

Pattern intelligence (grafted from the Pattern Intelligence proposal, gated hard). Four additive detector families read the V2 substrate after scoring, each keyed on (market, query_group), each gated by `channel_diversity` so a single-channel spike never reads as corroborated emergence. Emergence: a Kleinberg-style burst layer (cost-of-switching, threshold-free) plus a BQML `BOOSTED_TREE_CLASSIFIER` trained on the existing leak-safe `forecast_supervised` feature table with the label swapped to "became top-N within 7 days," catching a topic on the way up before it crosses the trending floor. Anomaly: online change-point (CUSUM/BOCPD posterior) to separate a durable regime shift from a one-day flare, plus a bubble discriminator that reads post-peak decay slope against `channel_diversity` and the search-leads-mentions lag (sharp spike, fast revert, single channel is exogenous bubble; gradual ramp, slow power-law relax, multi-channel is organic). Cross-market: a lead-lag scan over the partition-by-date, cluster-by-market `trend_scores` panel to catch a pattern bursting in ZA then diffusing to NG/KE, guarded by the endo/exo shape so a continent-wide exogenous shock is not mis-read as organic diffusion. Lifecycle: a fitted power-law/exponential decay on the post-peak `item_count` trajectory upgrading V2's threshold rules into a quantified half-life ("cold in N days"), handling re-ignition via the existing `continuity` rebounding state.

The hard rule: every detector ships dark, logs to a `detections_archive` cloned from `predictions_archive`, and must beat persistence/velocity on a trailing 4-week backtest AND clear a >90%-single-bucket degeneracy check before it renders. This is the exact gate that caught the live forecast chip running 92% "steady" (mathematically hugging persistence, decision-useless). Forecast itself stays dark: it lost to persistence twice and only re-flips on a non-degenerate win.

Intelligent data navigation, all inside BigQuery so client data never leaves GCP. V3 adds semantic retrieval over the corpus using the same already-paid-for M4 `text-multilingual-embedding-002`: one embedding column on `enriched_content` via `ML.GENERATE_EMBEDDING`, one GA `VECTOR_INDEX`, `VECTOR_SEARCH` fused in app code with the existing lexical `search_content`. Keep the geo-exclusion and topic-fit re-ranking a pure vector recall would lose, and a 0.65 threshold plus 0.05 margin (reused from the classifier) because short social text compresses cosines to a ~0.6 noise floor. Embed only `enriched_content`, not the 90-day-TTL `raw_content` firehose; reuse the content-hash cache discipline. No external vector store, no always-on Vertex Vector Search endpoint (wrong cost shape for sporadic desk volume).

Cost, when, and freshness are sized, not asserted as bounded. `ML.GENERATE_EMBEDDING` bills per call, and `enriched_content` grows daily, so the embed runs as a delta job over only the new content-hashes each day (the content-hash cache means a row is embedded once, ever), keeping daily embed volume to one day of new enriched rows, not the full corpus. It runs OUT of the 00:30 UTC critical-path cron, as a separate post-cron job, so it never competes with brief generation for the run window the rest of the blueprint guards. The cost line folds into the `vertex-cost-watchdog` so it trips the same $30 RED tripwire if it ever runs away. Index lag is a stated correctness fact, not just a cost note: a GA `VECTOR_INDEX` refreshes asynchronously, so this-morning's rows may not be in the index for a short window after they land. The fusion query therefore always runs the lexical `search_content` (which has no index lag) alongside `VECTOR_SEARCH` and unions the results, so a brand-new row is always reachable lexically even before the vector index catches up. The desk never silently misses this-morning's data because of index staleness.

The analyst-agent navigation already exists and is grafted from the Agentic Analyst proposal: the Listening Post `src/api/chat.py` grounded tool-calling loop (force-tools `mode=ANY` opening round so it cannot brief from memory, never-from-memory-numbers, degrade to "data is thin," fixed verified-query tools) and the Console Research evidence-graph plus quality gate. V3 promotes it to a first-class Ask plus proactive-scan surface, adds one `semantic_search` tool alongside the lexical one, and routes every retrieved row through the same evidence-ref and quality-gate path so a vector hit still carries a citable row id back to the receipt. Raw NL2SQL stays off the asserted path (SOTA text-to-SQL is ~1-in-5 wrong); it surfaces only as an analyst-facing draft that shows its SQL behind a verified-query allowlist. The `event_ledger` itself becomes a navigable time-travel surface: the LP can show how an event's state evolved day over day, read straight from the persisted ledger with no re-derivation. Every retrieved row that becomes a reader-facing claim crosses the reconcile boundary, so navigation and assertion share one trust contract.

## 6. Relevance, how the read always matters

Grafted from the Relevance Layer proposal. Relevance is two-axis and the axes are never collapsed. Loudness (the existing composite `trend_score`: velocity 0.20, genz 0.17, watchlist 0.12, engagement, slang, diversity, regional, creator_spread, search_velocity, tone) ranks what to LOOK AT. Defensibility (the spine's factual/social corroboration, event-state, and grounding verdict) decides what may be ASSERTED and how. A topic can be loud and undefensible: it ships as a social-signal observation with a single-source receipt, never as a fact. A topic can be quiet and highly defensible: it ships with a "factual N channels agree" receipt.

A deterministic re-rank sits between scoring and surfacing, computed in pure Python over already-persisted columns, never by an LLM, never mutating `trend_score`: R = audience_fit x trust_factor x novelty x usefulness_prior, all in [0,1], multiplicative so any factor near zero kills relevance. audience_fit reuses the `seed_score` audience_fit decomposition plus genz/slang/regional/watchlist into one "how much this matters to OUR SSA Gen Z book" score, market-segmented (KE leans on its live sources since `bigquery_trends` has zero KE coverage). trust_factor is a monotone map of the corroboration two-numbers and the reconcile event-state, where social-only caps below factual-corroborated and a reconcile STALE flag hard-demotes. novelty is the velocity windows plus the new burst flag and the endo/exo decay heuristic, rewarding genuine emergence over an evergreen that pegs the board daily. usefulness_prior is the one learned term, a bounded per-(market, topic-family, archetype) weight nudged by a lightweight team feedback signal (thumbs/clicks in the Listening Post), updated weekly within the `engine_evolve` plus/minus 20%-per-cycle bound with a hard floor and ceiling so a quiet week or a brigaded signal cannot swing the read. Cold start is handled explicitly so an undefined factor never silences a real signal: usefulness_prior defaults to exactly 1.0 (a no-op in the multiplicative R), and the learning loop stays OFF until at least N feedback events accumulate per (market, topic-family) cell (N set when the capture surface ships, on the order of a few dozen). Until that threshold, R is effectively the deterministic three-factor product with usefulness_prior pinned at 1.0, so a brand-new system with zero feedback history orders the board exactly as it would without the learned term, never on a near-zero undefined factor.

The reconcile action is the relevance gate on assertion. no_match or social-only renders as social signal or stays silent (silence beats a guess). corroborated asserts with a clickable receipt. stale corrects only behind the require-a-source gate, else labels. Crucially, for emerging thin-but-real signals the boundary DOWNGRADES to social rather than hard-DROPS, reserving DROP for contradicted or zero-evidence claims, so the recall cost of strictness does not silence genuine new trends. Corroboration is weighted by independent CHANNEL FAMILIES, not raw platform strings, so a single Brand24 feed emitting web/facebook/tiktok/instagram can never read as multi-source agreement. Recency is first-class via the 48h half-life and the ledger `validity_window`, so a stale retrospective cannot present as today's read.

## 8. Phased rollout

Each phase ships code dark behind a `cron_flags.env` flag, proves the OFF path byte-identical by unit test, lands writer-first (TEV2) before reader (Listening Post) so the reader never queries a missing column, and clears its watchdog gate before flip. Each phase is one reversible flag.

Writer-first protects the reader from a missing column, but not from a column that exists but is empty during the dark window, nor from a reconcile schema change the reader assumed a shape for while an old reader is still live (the Listening Post is a separate repo that auto-deploys on push, so the two deploy clocks can skew). The handshake that closes this: `render_payload` carries a `schema_version` integer, the Listening Post read path checks it and falls back to its prior-version render when it sees a newer payload it does not yet understand, and a column in its dark window reads as absent (the reader treats empty-and-dark identically to missing and renders the V2 surface). So a writer migration that lands while an old reader is live is degraded-but-correct, never broken. Disaster recovery runs the separate Intelligence Core migrations (`event_ledger.sql`, `reconcile_actions.sql`, already in `setup_bigquery.py` `SCHEMA_ORDER`) so a fresh dataset has the tables before either repo queries them.

Phase 0 (operational, week 1, no reader-facing change). Fix the foolproofness prerequisites: route every watchdog and live query through `get_dataset()` (kill the 14 hardcoded `trends_v2_dev` hits in `accuracy_watchdog.py` and the rest), make `dry_run_sql` REGISTRY completeness a test, add the unified per-day EnsembleData token ledger and the idempotent re-ingestion guard.

Then resume the shadow, with eyes open about why it stopped. The reconcile shadow is not merely paused awaiting a restart. It was turned ON in PR #184 (27 Jun), it ran, and it was deliberately turned back OFF in PR #201 titled "pause reconcile shadow + revert brief-cap raise" for cost. So flipping `RECONCILE_ENABLED` back on without first changing the cost shape repeats the exact decision that killed it last time. The cost figure is not a free-floating assertion: the reconcile ledger pass adds one Vertex Gemini call per (market, watched-entity) on the cron's critical path, which on roughly 24 briefs across three markets is the round-trip volume that PR #201 judged not worth it. Phase 0 therefore resumes the shadow ONLY after gating the ledger pass to Key-tier and Watch-tier entities and confirming the gated pass holds well under the $30 `vertex-cost-watchdog` RED line, rather than re-running the un-gated pass that was already rejected once. The "~$8 to 15/mo" headroom is a target to confirm against the gated shadow's actual measured spend in week one, not a given. Surface Stage-1 corroboration (already live and free, no Vertex cost) as an internal-only receipt to start calibrating its first-pass weights against a held window in parallel.

The idempotency guard has a deliberate override, because a hard guard would otherwise break the incident-recovery runbook. The documented V2 reality is that an incident recovery re-runs the pipeline and re-ingests by design to rebuild a failed day, so a guard that flatly refused re-ingestion would block legitimate recovery. The guard is therefore default-on for the normal cron path and bypassed by an explicit `FORCE_REINGEST` flag that the recovery runbook sets. Normal double-fires are blocked (no double-spend against the 5000 cap); a deliberate recovery re-ingests as it must, and the recovery runbook is updated to set the flag.

Promotion criteria: dataset routing green in dev and prod, registry test passing, the gated shadow producing a clean `reconcile_shadow_digest` daily, and week-one measured Vertex spend confirmed under the cost line.

Phase 1 (labels, add-only). After a clean ~10-day shadow window with the digest reviewed daily, zero receipt-exist failures and zero social-only-verified, ship the `claim_receipt` chip to the email and LP as labels (factual N / social M, as of <time>). The spine annotates but does not yet correct or suppress. Promote the calibrated corroboration weights. Promotion criteria: a test asserts the chip's denominator counts `_channel_family` collapsed families and that a synthetic single-Brand24-feed topic reads as one family not four (the family map already collapses this on `master`; the gate is a test that proves it stays collapsed end to end into the chip); `accuracy_watchdog` UNVERIFIED tier green.

Phase 2 (grounding verifier in shadow, then live, plus ledger deepening). Run the per-claim grounding pass in shadow, logging supported/partial/unsupported verdicts plus the resolved supporting row ids to an archive, backtest that it catches real ungrounded claims, then flip it to demote-or-cut. Widen ledger factual sourcing to YouTube and music. Add `validity_window` plus `current_state` supersession. Promotion criteria are measurable, not eyeballed: a hand-labelled ground-truth set of supported-vs-unsupported claims is built first (the desk labels roughly 100 to 200 claims drawn from the shadow archive, each adjudicated against its actual source rows, the same rows the verifier saw, so the human is checking the verifier against the evidence and not against a second model); the gate is zero unsupported-claim escapes AND a stated maximum false-cut rate on that labelled set, so over-strictness is measured too; deterministic `key_metrics` confirmed as the backstop on a verifier-outage test; latency measured to fit the run window for the gated tier set.

Phase 3 (drop/correct, the trust boundary goes live). Reconcile's stale-correction and the render-time future-tense validator gate live output. A resolved event can no longer be asserted as upcoming. Promotion criteria use a defined detection method, not a human eyeballing: a shadow diff logs every claim where the live boundary WOULD change the rendered text (correct, label, drop), so the reviewer audits a finite, surfaced list of every intervention rather than scanning whole briefs; the gate is human sign-off that the surfaced interventions contain zero true-to-false inversions (a correction that made a true claim false) over the window, with the future-tense validator's own fired/not-fired log as the second check on the same set.

Phase 4 (THE READ swap, retrieval, detectors, relevance reorder). Swap THE READ to the ledger-reconciled read with Brand24 demoted to a dated social lens. Ship the `VECTOR_SEARCH` hybrid retrieval and the `semantic_search` LP tool. Promote the pattern detectors out of dark only after each beats persistence/velocity on its `detections_archive` backtest and clears the degeneracy gate. Flip the relevance re-rank so the board orders by `relevance_rank`; keep V2 ordering one flag-flip away for instant rollback. Activate the bounded `usefulness_prior` feedback loop. Each sub-step is its own shadow-then-promote.

## 9. The foolproof guarantees

Ten rails, each enforced in code or by process, not by prose.

No-Verified honesty rail (hard). `confidence_label` only ever returns "single-source" or "N channels agree," never "Verified"/"Confirmed." Every chip names what it literally measures (LOCAL is a filter pass-rate, not geo-trust). Enforced in a pure tested function.

Corrector-is-never-the-trust-boundary (architectural). The generative model only PROPOSES; reconcile is 100% deterministic with zero Gemini and disposes. A model state is dropped unless it cites a real evidence row; the deterministic guess wins ties; confidence is always computed in Python.

Require-a-source gate (hard). A stale claim is corrected to verbatim event state ONLY with at least 2 factual families AND confidence at least 0.55, otherwise labelled. Reconcile never writes free text.

Silence beats a guess. No ledger entry means no correction and the claim ships date-stamped. no_match or zero-evidence renders as social signal or not at all. DROP is reserved for contradicted/zero-evidence; emerging thin signals DOWNGRADE to social rather than vanish. The fixed-cadence artifacts (the daily digest and THE READ ship every morning regardless) need a defined low-corroboration day so silence does not produce a near-empty client email. On a thin day the digest still ships the ranked board ordered by loudness, but every topic that downgrades to social carries the honest social-signal framing and chip; the email never goes empty because loudness ranking always has content, it just leads with "today is a social-signal day, here is what is moving with thin factual corroboration" rather than asserting facts it cannot defend. For BSA specifically, an all-hedged morning is a known acceptable state agreed with the client up front, not a surprise, so a thin day reads as honest restraint, not a broken product. The digest template carries a thin-day header variant so the framing is built in, not improvised by the model.

Two-numbers law. `factual_corroboration` and `social_corroboration` are never combined; social-only can NEVER reach corroborated; the denominator counts independent channel FAMILIES, never platform strings, so single-vendor multi-platform cannot fake corroboration.

Every number computed by code. `_clamp_status_tag`, `_build_key_metrics`, and the HARD PICK velocity rule already do this; V3 makes it total. The LLM owns prose only.

Provenance or it does not render. `assert_sourced` extends from value-exists to source-pointer-exists; a rendered claim that was generated with no backing `receipt_id` raises (a bug), it does not degrade. This is distinct from a receipt whose backing enriched row later ages out past the 90-day TTL: that is not a missing receipt, it is a known-aged one, so the chip renders as a non-clickable "source aged out" label rather than raising or dangling a dead link. Span-level binding, because a document-level citation can pass while the exact span does not support the claim.

Testing and QA. The unit suite (roughly 1550 test functions across about 70 files as of this snapshot, and growing) is the Cloud Build deploy gate (a red build never deploys). `dry_run_sql` validates real SQL server-side at zero cost before merge and as a morning-check pre-flight, with REGISTRY completeness now a test. `composite_integrity` recomputes every `trend_score`; V3 adds a `relevance_integrity` recompute and a grounding-faithfulness check on the same template. `audit-eml` and `morning-check` stay the daily human-grade QA on the live artifact.

Shadow discipline. No new judging, confidence, correction, or detector surface reaches a client until a clean ~10-day audit window with the digest reviewed daily, the watchdog green, and human sign-off on zero true-to-false inversions. Self-judging bias is mitigated three ways: deterministic `key_metrics` stay the real backstop, a grounding verdict only passes when it resolves to a real `receipt_id`, and the promotion gate is measured against a human-labelled set adjudicated on the actual source rows rather than against a second model.

Cost guardrails. All-Google/Vertex only, so client data stays in the WPP/GCP boundary (no third-party model API on client data). The project-level $50 Cloud Billing budget alert is the outer bound; the `vertex-cost-watchdog` ($30 RED) is the finer tripwire and V3 extends it to cover the new agent, embedding, and judge spend. The ledger pass and the grounding pass run shadow-first to confirm they do not push the 00:30 UTC run past its window; self-consistency sampling is gated to Key-tier topics; embedding is bounded to `enriched_content` only and runs out of the critical-path cron; the idempotency guard stops a normal double-fire from breaching the 5000 EnsembleData cap, while a deliberate incident recovery sets `FORCE_REINGEST` to re-ingest as the runbook requires (the guard protects against accidental double-spend, it does not block legitimate recovery).

## 10. Risks and mitigations

Self-judging bias. The grounding verifier is a Vertex Gemini call (there is no pre-built "check-grounding model" in the stack, so this is Gemini grading Gemini, ~+3.2% optimistic per FACTS Grounding), and WPP-Open compliance forbids a non-Google second judge so the usual ensemble cancellation is unavailable. This is the most novel risk in V3 and the plan does not lean on the judge alone. Mitigation, three layers: deterministic `key_metrics`/`status_tag` stay the real backstop and the verifier gates prose only; a verdict only passes a claim when it resolves to a real `receipt_id` for that topic, so the model cannot pass a claim by asserting grounding without naming a row that exists; the Phase 2 gate is measured against a human-labelled set adjudicated on the same source rows, not against a second model, with both a zero-escape and a max-false-cut bound. The judge prompt is adversarial.

Claim decomposition is its own hard problem, not a free seam. Splitting "X is rising because Y" into the two claims it contains is classic NLP failure territory: a naive sentence split grounds a compound as one unit and passes a half-false sentence or cuts a half-true one. Mitigation: split to clause level on conjunction boundaries, ground each clause independently, and on any decomposition the splitter is unsure about, fall back to grounding the whole sentence and DEMOTING on a mixed verdict rather than cutting, so the conservative failure is hedged phrasing and never an asserted half-truth. Decomposition carries its own unit tests on hand-labelled compound sentences.

Over-claiming corroboration was a real failure mode and the structural fix already shipped. A flat multiplier could once fire on ~95% of topic-days and a single Brand24 feed could earn a false "confirmed across channels" bump. The fix is on `master`: `_channel_family` collapses every Brand24 platform string to one `brand24` family (fitted 2026-06-16) and `corroboration.py` counts collapsed families, so a single feed already cannot read as multi-source agreement. The residual risk is regression, not absence: a new connector or a renamed source could quietly default into the wrong family. Mitigation: the Phase 1 gate is a test that a synthetic single-Brand24-feed topic reads as one family all the way into the chip, so a future family-map regression fails the build before any chip over-claims.

Recency/staleness, the original sin. A vendor returning a weekly retrospective regardless of window can poison `current_state`. Mitigation: every event-state update is timestamped, `validity_window` enforced, reconcile re-verifies against fresh evidence each run and never caches a verdict.

Cost is the binding constraint, not correctness, and it already won once. The ledger's per-market Gemini pass plus a per-claim grounding pass across ~24 briefs adds Vertex round-trips on the cron's critical path; this is the exact spend PR #201 cut when it paused the shadow for cost. Resuming the un-gated pass would repeat that decision. Mitigation: do not resume un-gated. Gate the expensive checks to Key-tier and Watch-tier topics from the first shadow day, batch per topic, pre-filter, measure week-one spend against the $30 cost-watchdog tripwire before any further flip, and keep the grounding pass and the ledger pass shadow-first to confirm they fit the 00:30 UTC run window before they touch the live path.

Abstention has a recall cost. A strict DROP gate silences genuinely true but thinly-corroborated emerging topics. Mitigation: DOWNGRADE-to-social for emerging signals, reserve DROP for contradicted/zero-evidence.

Multiplicative R is unforgiving. A mis-scaled factor near zero could silence a real signal. Mitigation: emerging-but-thin caps a factor rather than zeroing it, and the `relevance_integrity` watchdog plus daily audit review catch over-suppression before reorder goes live.

The feedback loop is gameable and sparse. A small team's thumbs can be brigaded or quiet. Mitigation: bounded plus/minus 20%/cycle with floor and ceiling, feedback as nudge not gate, drift monitored in `engine_evolve`.

Pattern detectors on short, noisy, mean-reverting series. ~24-26 topics x 3 markets at a 7-day horizon is near the worst case for beating persistence, and the forecast already failed twice. Mitigation: detectors degrade gracefully to the new-topic path, ship dark, and must clear the backtest AND the degeneracy gate before they count; forward value comes from event-conditioning, not univariate curve-fit.

Semantic recall can re-leak geo. A pure vector search revives the Vietnam-sapa / KE-Sheng collisions the classifier fought. Mitigation: keep the geo-exclusion and topic-fit re-rank, apply threshold plus margin, fuse with lexical rather than replacing it, route every hit through the evidence/quality gate.

Two-repo span and substrate fragility. A receipt/retrieval change touches TEV2 (writer) and the separate Listening Post repo (CI auto-deploy), whose deploy clocks can skew; watchdogs still hardcode `trends_v2_dev` (14 hits in `accuracy_watchdog.py` alone). `setup_bigquery.py` already includes the Intelligence Core tables (`event_ledger.sql` and `reconcile_actions.sql` are in `SCHEMA_ORDER`), so that gap is closed; the live risk is the deploy-skew window, not missing tables. Mitigation: land writer-first behind the dark flag, route everything through `get_dataset()` as a Phase-0 prerequisite, carry a `schema_version` in `render_payload` so an old reader degrades to its prior render instead of breaking when a newer payload lands, and have the reader treat an empty-and-dark column identically to a missing one.

Confidence-anchoring on the human. A displayed confidence pulls an exec's own confidence toward it even when honestly worded. Mitigation: conservative defaults (single-source stays plain, no hot chip), surface uncertainty not just a point.
