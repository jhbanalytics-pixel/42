## Reading back against the delta

`diff_against_readback(delta, readback)` takes a mapping of resource name to native policy in the shape the retained readbacks use (`bindings` with `members`, or a BigQuery dataset `access` list, which it normalizes: `WRITER` to `roles/bigquery.dataEditor`, `READER` to `roles/bigquery.dataViewer`, and authorized routines to `routine:` members). It reports `present`, `missing`, `extra` (a manifest identity holding something the delta does not propose), `unexpected` (any other principal), `retained_present` and `retained_absent` for the app identity, and `existing_broad_grants_not_isolated` for grants that native discovery flagged: a project wide role from the broad set such as the existing `cloudbuild-deployer` `roles/run.developer` and `roles/artifactregistry.writer`, a whole bucket object role such as the app identity's `roles/storage.objectCreator` and `roles/storage.objectViewer`, a conditional bucket grant outside the allowlist such as the app identity's ledger replace, and a `roles/bigquery.routineDataEditor` authorization for a legacy routine. A refused read is never treated as an empty policy: the resource lands in `unproven` with reason `refused` and the count of bindings that could not be checked, and a resource the readback never covered lands there with reason `unread`.
