## 0. The short version

1. There are two regimes and they must never be joined into one series. Legacy (old engine, trends_v2_dev) holds about 1.78M enriched posts, 1.50M seed_graph term rows from 18 June 2026 and 4.5k trend_scores rows from 16 April 2026 (INV:13-35). The SocialCrawl regime (intelligence_42_core) started collecting on or about 28 September 2026 (inferred, section 1.3): **about 18 days of collection, and much less under the protocols now running.**
2. The live clean repo begins on 6 October (ecff3e2); history before that is in the archive repo and is not here. The protocol changes visible here cluster on 8 to 12 October (Release B, first daily run 12 October, CHANGELOG.md "2.1.0") and 13 to 15 October (Alpha 2.4). Several measured series therefore have **4 or fewer days under their current protocol** (section 2).
3. The series test has never switched on as far as any record shows (KX:164 "no record of the backtest or his yes"). So every series_test row is test 'none', no state is "Tested", Rising, Peaking, Fading and Mainstream cannot occur, early_signal writes no rows, and forecast targets reach_rising and cross_market can only resolve as non-arrivals (section 6). This is from code; verify with `series_test_states` and `span_test_switch`.
4. Topic identity (cultural_map topics) is not stable enough for longitudinal work. Live f9 lets recency count as an identity vote, so unrelated clusters chain into one item (KE item drifted forestry, spam, highway, HR complaint, heater over 13 to 16 October; 7 of 8 sampled transitions labelled not the same topic: identity-replay CSV). The Beta fixes (c6d84d6, f555cb4, 412b1ba, b7a6806) prevent some future bad matches and are not deployed; they repair nothing in history (CP:48-49).
5. Hashtag, sound and creator items are lexical ids (sha256 of kind and canonical key, DATA:124) and are stable over time. They are the only identities fit for longitudinal analysis today.
6. What Gamma can do now: descriptive, collection-aware counts on hashtag/sound/creator items within one protocol; first-seen dates with regime labels; legacy "memory" (was this term seen before 28 September) with heavy caveats. What it cannot do yet: lasting-versus-spike classification with a test, lead time, seasonality, cross-market diffusion claims, forecasting skill. Section 7 gives the verdict per use.

## 3. Coverage and gaps

Markets and platforms. Measured (baseline) lanes per DATA:204-227 and SOURCES.md:53-84: TikTok local feed (ZA, NG 3 pulls; KE 1, job.py:156); TikTok hashtag board ZA only (no NG or KE board from the supplier, KX:173); YouTube trending 5 categories per market but "country on YouTube trending ... not available" (KX:173) and foreign channels stamped local (KX:231); Apple Music charts ZA, NG and KE only if a probe passed (DATA:209); Reddit own subreddits ZA 6, NG 3, KE 3 (SOURCES:59); Facebook hub pages (4 per market); culture desk prism panel (12 accounts per market); X hub panel; GLOBAL counters. Telegram is off (TELEGRAM_READY False, core/collect/job.py:64). X trends archive (23b) is off until legal agrees (job.py:15) and is "never evidence, floor, state or Today" even when on (DATA:211). X creator timelines are archive-only from the supplier (KX:161), so panel_x_hub measures archive availability rather than posting rate (inferred). Google search is triage only, never post evidence or counts (RULES.md rule 2). The local charts lane is task 2.12 under a 40-credit sub-limit (job.py:18-22).

Biased versus unbiased lanes. Only unbiased_rank, unbiased_counter and panel form baselines; only unbiased_rank and panel posts meet floors; search_presence, watchlist and legacy never do (DATA:7, 230). But several readers do mix them: v_item_waves counts every lane class except '_any', including search_presence and legacy (waves.sql:2-4, 13-18), and the '_any' rows count every post once across all lanes except legacy and agent_live (DATA:83). Both therefore move when 42 searches more. Expansion searches are targeted at items already scoring high (DATA:769), so a search_presence count rises because 42 looked, not because culture moved: the textbook confusion between collection and change. The placebo lane (about 5% of expansions on random low items) exists to estimate that effect (DATA:224, 770) and is excluded from waves (waves.sql:6-7, 17).

Validity machinery. collection_health judges each market, series, protocol and day: calls invalid when under 80% succeed; items invalid outside half to twice the median of up to 28 prior valid days (only once 3 exist); effort invalid when a panel's k is outside 0.5 to 2 (core/collect/writers.py:599-611); zero_yield on the second of two zero days of a paid route (writers.py:513-524; DATA:270). The A1 feed drift check is **not built** (writers.py:22-23), and the A3 vendor regime change test (ruptures) does not exist in code (grep finds no ruptures or change-point code in core; TRUST:11 lists it as the guard). G1 holds an item when 2 of the last 3 market-days are invalid on its main platform, baseline lanes only (KX:88). Days before a series' protocol start and invalid days are NULL, never zero (DATA:8). Warm-up: no test before 14 observed days of one protocol (DATA:347; TRUST:99); thin when fewer than 14 of the last 28 are observed (DATA:348).

Known failed or odd days: YouTube outage 2 Oct (KX:175); Instagram location 503s (KX:232); NG App Store invalid 3 Oct (KX:233); a part-written clustering day cannot be repaired the same day (KX:234, F023); the 6 Oct Vertex 429 failures (KX:155); a collect run that fails after writing is retried whole and readers without an ok-run filter count both runs' rows, misaligning pull sequences and next-day deltas (DA:57). The per-day picture needs `health_by_day` and `ok_runs_by_day`.

Locality. A post is located only with geo_confidence 0.7 or more from ext.region, the creator's home market or a place mention; language alone never counts (DATA:35, 380). Sources: TikTok profile author.location, Instagram profile country, TikTok search and trending post.ext.region; TikTok profile/videos carry no country; YouTube, Shorts, Threads and Facebook carry none (KX:172-173). Instagram reel search with creator card adds author_country (job.py:143-150). Kenya: the feed is about 12% in-country (KX:230; job.py:152-153 records KE 1 of 8 in the probe); item eligibility admits market_unconfirmed items (fewer than 8 located posts in 7 days) because eligible tests only `geo_status != 'not_local'` (core/detect/sql/state.sql:175-176, 188-191; REC:52 corrects the export's ~178); KE showed South African topics (KX:215). Locality v2 exists only as shadow (core/trust/locality.py:38). Search `region=` is where the request is sent from, never a market (KX:110). So "market" in KE is often "seen through a KE-scoped route", not "made in Kenya". located_share per day is in collection_health (DATA:74) and is the thing to track over time (`located_share`).

Participation fields. creator_tier_at_post is frozen at first sighting from follower counts (DATA:28); tier is NULL where the route gives no follower count, and legacy rows leave tier NULL (DATA:165). item_daily.tier_posts holds tier counts per item-day (DATA:86). The creators MERGE is switched off (DATA:140), so home_market and coord_score coverage may be thin (inferred).

Post identity. One post can get two post_ids when one route gives a native id and another only a URL (DA:58), which double counts posts seen on two routes and inflates cross-route "spread" (inferred). The 1970 date bug (`parse_time` reads 0 and YYYYMMDD strings as epoch seconds) is fixed only on the audit branch, not here (core/collect/parse.py:361-368 still converts any digit string; DA:22). So posts.post_date and published_at can hold 1970 dates, which bias breakout recency, diffusion timing (small_at, large_at) and young_share (inferred; `post_dates`).

## 4. Identity drift

How identity forms (core/understand/cluster.py; DATA:751-756).
- Each night per market and for a pooled 'pan' run, BERTopic fits three days of posts on stored embeddings; only posts sighted today become members (DATA:753). Minimum 30 posts (cluster.py:87).
- Each cluster is compared with the top 3 cultural_map items by centroid cosine (cluster.py:93). Votes: cosine at least 0.82, keyword Jaccard at least 0.10, a shared specific hashtag or sound, a shared creator, and on f9 a "recent" vote for an item seen in 7 days (cluster.py:491-507). Matching needs two votes besides "recent" and at least one of cosine, keywords or hashtag (cluster.py:520-523 on this branch; on f9 recency counted, c6d84d6). One-to-one Hungarian assignment (cluster.py:526-532).
- A match to an item last seen 28 or more days ago is a recurrence (cluster.py:99, 481-482, 541) and increments cultural_map.recurrences (cluster.py:631). Cosine 0.70 to 0.82 makes a variant child item with parent_item_id (cluster.py:546-548, 639). Anything else is a new item keyed topic:<cluster_id> (cluster.py:635-643).
- Matched items keep their label (cluster.py:609-617); labels change only under the v2 locality authority with 2 shared members (c7c8bdf), and v2 is not live (locality.py:38). Centroids update by EMA 0.8 old, 0.2 new (cluster.py:100, 475-478). Hashtag and sound centroids are separate: the mean of their posts' embeddings over a window, versioned in cultural_map (core/detect/centroids.py docstring).

Consequences for longitudinal work.
1. Topic identity is path dependent: the EMA centroid drifts with each match, so the item a cluster is compared with on day 20 is not the item of day 1. With labels frozen under v1, an item that drifted keeps its first title, which is the "mixed clusters under good titles" problem (KX:216; RC:78 counts 8 of 30 held topics as incoherent clusters).
2. Known contamination: recency plus cosine chained unrelated clusters (KE forestry to heater, 4 transitions at cosine 0.83 to 0.97 with keyword Jaccard 0; ZA item 3 transitions likewise, 1 genuine: /mnt/project-files/42-beta/identity-replay/model-assisted-labels-20261016.csv, labels "provisional model-assisted, not human validation"). Blank BERTopic keywords gave any two caption-less clusters a keyword vote (DA:13). Shared publisher plus generic keywords still merges after all fixes (CP:37, 48). A topic HDBSCAN splits in two becomes two identities, because a cluster at cosine 0.82 or more to an item it did not win becomes a new parentless item (DA:52). Same-event fragments are separate items (one Champions League fixture became 4 items: RC:21).
3. Recurrence is unavailable for topics in the SocialCrawl regime: a recurrence needs an item dormant 28 days, and topics only exist from about the first cluster run (early October at the latest, RC:78), so `recurrences` should be near 0 for topics now (inferred; `cultural_map_summary`). Recurring as a state comes from v_item_waves, not from cluster recurrence (state.sql:73-80, 160), and for hashtags it can be produced by a legacy wave followed by a SocialCrawl wave, i.e. by the regime boundary (inferred).
4. cultural_map rows are versioned by valid_from and valid_to (DATA:130; centroids.py docstring), so the identity state on a past date can in principle be reconstructed; but cluster_items reads keywords from the item's latest cluster only (core/understand/sql/cluster_items.sql:7), and the identity decision depends on the whole chain.
5. Preventing future bad matches is not repairing history. The Beta fixes change cluster.assign for new runs only; existing chained items keep their merged post history, merged item_daily rows, item_state and v_item_waves. "Historical contaminated identities need a separate reviewed, reversible data plan" (CP:49). For research, the honest options are (a) restrict to lexical items, (b) treat each daily cluster as its own observation and re-link offline with the fixed rule from stored clusters, centroids and members (identity_replay already does the transition part, core/eval/identity_replay.py), keeping both linkages, or (c) wait for clean post-fix history.

## 5. Measurement consistency

Comparable over time within one market (with conditions):
- Rank list appearances (feed, boards, charts, Reddit) within one protocol: fixed length and pulls (DATA:204-210, 225), tested by presence. Not volumes: "post counts in a fixed-length list are small and zero-sum" (TRUST:94). The feed is shaped by the vendor account (TRUST:9, A1) and drift is not monitored (writers.py:22).
- GLOBAL counters (daily deltas of totals) from an item's first read: a counter series starts at the first read, earlier days NULL (DATA:231); a negative delta is a vendor correction set to 0 (DATA:365); NULL when the previous day is missing (DATA:258). These are the closest thing to an unbiased volume, but platform-wide, not per market (DATA:223).
- Panel posts per day times k, only within one membership protocol and only for days with k in [0.5, 2] (DATA:234).

Not comparable:
- Across markets on the feed: KE has 1 pull, ZA and NG 3 (job.py:156), so trials and appearance rates differ; KE's feed is 12% in-country.
- Panels across protocols (membership hash, v2 tokens, rotation), across markets (different accounts), and the culture desk before and after 10 Oct (ce341dd).
- Any '_any', search_presence, watchlist or wave height over time, because search effort scales with credits and is retuned weekly by yield (DATA:767).
- Vendor curves: a curve read on first sighting carries past days as source 'vendor_history', which "count as observed days of that series" (DATA:231), and the newest read of each value wins (DATA:184-189), so a past day's value can be restated by a later read. A backtest must filter available_at (DATA:168), which backtest.py does (backtest.py docstring); naive reads of v_item_counter_daily_current do not.
- item_daily before and after 13 Oct (d10eb7b link rule) and any derived table across rule_version tokens.
- Legacy vs SocialCrawl heights (DATA:168).
- Enrichment-based shares (formats, sounds, sponsored, tone) across the 3 Oct limit change and across days, because enrichment is a capped selection that prefers posts the brief's candidates rest on (core/understand/sql/enrich_select.sql:11).

NULL versus zero. Zero is written only for a valid day on which a list or panel that watches everything did not see the item (DATA:8, 231). Before protocol start and on invalid days the value is NULL. Any Gamma analysis that fills NULLs with 0, or that treats "not collected" as "absent", invents decline and emergence. collection_health is the only record of which days count.
