# App and API: standing decisions

Decisions from Albert that the app (app/frontend) and the API (core/api) follow. The specification is core/api/contract.md; caps are in SETUP.md.

- Gemini on Vertex for Ask: core/api/deploy_flags.env sets GEMINI_MODEL=gemini-3.8-flash for f42-agent.
- Deploys: the app and API deploy to staging only, from a reviewed commit, with `bash core/api/deploy.sh --local-build` as f42-builder. f42-api is reachable behind the passcode gate until IAP is on (SETUP.md, "Who can open 42").
- Creator pages: only creators at macro tier (500,000 followers and up) get a named page; everyone else appears only in counts.
- Suppression list: f42-api reads it through the store's suppressed_creators on every route that names a person.
- Scheduled questions: SCHEDULED_DAILY is 120 credits, a share of ASK_DAILY (core/config/caps.yaml, read by core/api/scheduled.py).
- Email digest: f42-digest sends only when DIGEST_RECIPIENTS and DIGEST_SEND_ENABLED are set on the job (SETUP.md).
- Team channel post: not wired. No webhook is wanted (contract 14.3).
- Client skins: an approved client's organisation accounts and terms are entered in core/api/skin_accounts.yaml exactly as given; until then a skin accepts no accounts.
- Creator brand-fit: reports fit only. Audience figures stay aggregate and non-accusing, with no category touching religion, race or ethnicity, health, politics, sex life or crime, and nothing that attributes coordination or payment.
- Sign-in: IAP waits on whether Ogilvy South Africa staff sign in with Google or Microsoft accounts (SETUP.md); a read-only IAP pilot is described in core/api/IAP-PILOT.md.
