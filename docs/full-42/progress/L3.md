## Country names used for people (DEMO-03)

A country name used to mean the people who live there ("Kenyans are", "South Africans think") needs a cited post actually located in that market. Posts from another market and source-only feeds cannot establish that population. Ask must not say what a nationality thinks or does unless a cited post says it, and a creator's home market or a source-feed tag does not establish a named person's nationality. The support and field checks in core/agent/checks.py classify these uses; an invalid or missing classification stops the answer with its spend kept on the ledger. The paired evaluation question DEMO-03 (core/eval/demo_pairs.py) tests it, and core/agent/checks.py treats a nationality attached to a named person as a claim that needs a cited post whose own text states it.
