# 42, the full build

42 is Ogilvy South Africa's cultural trend engine. Every morning it crawls every channel it can reach in South Africa, Nigeria and Kenya, works out what is taking off, confirms it, explains it with posts you can open, and answers any strategist's question with cited claims.

Read in this order:

1. SPEC.md: what 42 is, the rules, the decisions and what done means.
2. ENGINE.md: the morning trend engine, the schedule, all channels, the detection scorecard.
3. TRUST.md: how 42 earns trust, and the gate that blocks anything unsupported.
4. BRAIN.md and AGENT.md: how 42 thinks and the analyst agent in detail.
5. SOURCES.md: SocialCrawl and every other source, and the seed loop.
6. DATA.md: tables and detection maths.
7. EXPERIENCE.md and FEATURES.md: the screens and every feature by stage.
8. SETUP.md: cloud, permissions once, deploys, access, cost guards.
9. SALVAGE.md: what carries over from the old 42 and the earlier build branches.
10. BUILD.md and PROGRESS.md: the full order of work with a check for every task, and where it stands.
11. RULES.md: the rules every change to 42 follows.

reference/sc_routes.json is a compact copy of SocialCrawl's live API specification (every route, its credits and parameters) as downloaded on 28 September 2026; the full spec is at https://www.socialcrawl.dev.
